Tell Capri: type like a text, e.g. math mon wed 8 to 9:30, physics tue thu 10:30-12, move physics to 1pm, remove math, what's tomorrow. Times 1–6 without am/pm count as afternoon. Or tap a sample above the chatbox.
Week grid: tap an empty spot to add a class (1 hour), drag a class to move it, drag its bottom edge to make it longer, tap it to rename, copy to other days or delete. Both modes share the same timetable.
