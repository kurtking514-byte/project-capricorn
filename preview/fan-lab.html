What each switch does

Side strip: slide your thumb up and down the letters on the right edge. Bottom keys: tap or slide along the letters under her. Spin the ring: turn it like a dial by moving your finger around her head, or tap a letter to bring it to the top. Tap an app to open it. Press Open the fan to replay how it appears. The head is a plain stand-in shape; the app uses your skin. 40 made-up apps so you can feel a full phone.
