What it is: one SETUP screen with every permission Capri can use. Tap a crimson ALLOW: in the app that opens Android's own page or pop-up (simulated here by the grey box); when you come back it snaps to a green ON with a short glitch, a segment in the bar lights up, and she reacts in the box at the top.

When: it opens once after the very first boot, and any time you type setup. LATER and ✕ just close it; nothing nags you afterwards. Only HOME APP is marked NEEDED; everything else is optional and the feature just stays off. When all 10 are on, she says so and DONE turns into the only button.

New, step 4.5: BATTERY. Three rows at the bottom so Realme's battery cleaner leaves her alone (mini Capri, face-down silent and notification reactions run in the background). Battery saver: Android's own pop-up; it turns ON by itself. Auto launch: opens Capri's app info → Battery usage (the grey box); flip both switches, come back, and the chip turns into a yellow DONE?. Tap it to mark it, since Realme doesn't tell Capri. Lock in recents: nothing opens; she tells you how, and the chip turns into DONE? for you to tap.
