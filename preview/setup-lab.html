What it is: one SETUP screen with every permission Capri can use. Tap a crimson ALLOW: in the app that opens Android's own page or pop-up (simulated here by the grey box); when you come back it snaps to a green ON with a short glitch, a segment in the bar lights up, and she reacts in the box at the top.

When: it opens once after the very first boot, and any time you type setup. LATER and ✕ just close it; nothing nags you afterwards. Only HOME APP is marked NEEDED; everything else is optional and the feature just stays off. When all 7 are on, she says so and DONE turns into the only button.
