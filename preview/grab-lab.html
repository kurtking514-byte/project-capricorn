How the grab works

    Drag any app (dock or "from search") onto her with your mouse or finger. Drop it anywhere else and it snaps back. Tap outside the menu to cancel. The hit timing is the app's command hit (C3), the strips + bars are the pose change you picked (D4). The figure is a plain stand-in; the app uses your skin.
